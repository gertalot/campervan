Fix the demonstrated faults without moving the scribed plywood or changing van fit, bays, module bolts or doors.

1. Trace actual fixed hinge leaves and catch bodies against frame faces. Keep hardware envelopes clearly distinct
   from manufacturer drilling dimensions. Preserve existing door motion paths and four visibility filters.
2. Remove wide 140mm corner uprights and five short rear-strip rails. Use an ordinary 40 × 18mm wall-side end
   post at the main back frame; retain the shallow rear end frame. The frames connect through the front rails,
   transverse frames and scribed shelves, rather than a tiny stepped corner assembly. No plywood setback.
3. Add identical 40 × 40 × 18mm mounting blocks behind the front headers for catches, with real bearing/contact.
   Put each catch mounting plate against the block underside and align its keeper on the door. Model the fixed
   hinge/frame fixings and preserve full timber at flap-hinge screw positions with local shelf notches.
