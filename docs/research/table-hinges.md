# Folding tabletop hinges

Researched 27 October 2026 for the current two **700 × 250 × 25mm bamboo leaves**. One leaf folds up
onto the other along the 700mm seam. The open leaf projects 250mm beyond that seam; the Lagun plate
attaches to the fixed leaf.

**Gert selected the [Homer HOM006136 stainless folding-table hinge pair](https://fostersshipchandlery.co.nz/products/table-hinge-homer-pair)
on 27 October 2026.** Fosters lists $75.67 for two hinges and describes a dual-pin mechanism that supports
the tabletop horizontally and lets it fold over 180°. Published dimensions are 114mm open length, 42mm
maximum width and 38mm pin width. Capacity with our bamboo leaf and fixings still needs establishing.
The BOM records two hinges as one pair to buy. The options below retain the research comparison.

## Fitting them to our bamboo top

The CAD shows two top-recessed Homer hinges, with centres at 20% and 80% along the seam. Each hinge bridges
the joint and finishes flush with the open working surface. Its centre mechanism allows the second leaf
to fold face-to-face onto the fixed leaf. The model retains the simple seam rotation for the bamboo and
attaches each metal half to its leaf. It illustrates the centre links without reproducing the physical
dual-pin mechanism's movement. Final positions and articulation require the hardware.

The model uses Fosters' 114 × 42mm open footprint and 38mm pin width. Its 3mm metal thickness, 4mm pivot
offsets, 3mm barrel radii, taper, corner radii and eight-hole layout are photo-based approximations. The
nominal seats show how the hinges fit; they provide no routing template. The model leaves bamboo pilot
holes unspecified, and the existing 50mm folded wood stack and 127mm stored assembly remain previews.

Machining needs shallow pockets for the metal leaves and deeper clearance for their central mechanism.
A router with a straight cutter and a chisel can make these recesses. The sources above do not specify
the complete pocket profile, centre depth, screw diameter/length or required joint gap. Use the selected
hardware to establish those dimensions before cutting the finished top. A sample joint in bamboo offcuts
can establish flushness and folding clearance.

The nominal seats interrupt the 3mm round-over at each hinge. The seam retains its round-over between
hinges. Establish the actual bearing surfaces and any further edge relief from the selected hardware
before machining the bamboo.

The Lagun plate occupies 200mm of the fixed leaf's 250mm width, leaving 25mm between its edge and the seam.
For initial footprint planning, the selected 114mm Homer hinge centred on that seam reaches about 57mm
into the fixed leaf. Its footprint therefore overlaps the underside plate by about 32mm wherever their
lengthwise positions coincide. Confirm its exact position from the hardware's central mechanism. They mount
on opposite faces, but their fixing locations and screw lengths need coordinating. Final hinge positions
must account for the selected Lagun mounting offset and its actual fixing holes.

None of the shortlisted suppliers publishes a permitted leaf size, load or deflection for this installation.
The support mechanism addresses the fold-over function; the strength of our unsupported 250mm leaf remains
unverified. Two hinges remain a proposed arrangement. If the selected pair and bamboo fixings do not provide
enough stiffness for laptop use, add under-leaf support designed around the Lagun plate and rotating arm.

## Other fittings considered

[Häfele 341.32.708 / 341.32.502](https://files.hafele.co.uk/CatalogFiles/www/18T9-167.PDF) are purpose-made
self-supporting folding-table hinges. The manufacturer's section shows 65 × 30mm overall size, 2mm face
thickness and an 8mm central depth. It shows both the flat-open and face-to-face folded arrangements and
allows timber thickness to suit the application. I found no confirmed NZ stock or retail price, so they
remain outside the purchasing shortlist under the no-special-orders requirement.

Ordinary butt/piano hinges would need a separate support for the open leaf. Thickness-specific butterfly
extension hinges add machining and procurement complications. Neither offers a demonstrated advantage over
the stocked folding-table fittings above for this design.

The selected product is Homer HOM006136. Fabrication recesses, hole positions, joint gap and the
installation's load capacity remain unresolved.
