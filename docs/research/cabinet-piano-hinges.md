The 27mm open width is published. Neither the retailer nor Taskmaster's supplier listing provides a
dimensioned hinge section or fixing-hole offsets. Product photos do not establish accurate dimensions.
The CAD preview uses nominal 0.8mm leaves, 2mm pin, 3.6mm barrel and the current 2mm door gap.
These are explicitly not verified product dimensions or drilling templates. Physical hinge measurement
must establish gap, mortices, screw row and screw lengths. The unchanged rectangular plywood doors retain
their glued 18mm-square backing strips. A screw row close to a sound, fully bonded glue line is not equivalent
to a free timber edge. Glue and clamp the full mating face, allow full cure, then accurately pilot-drill the
assembled edge. No rebates in the plywood or separate solid-wood door edging are required.
No speculative fixing holes or hinge screws are modelled.

The Taskmaster product photo shows a bag of mounting screws included with the hinge. Their dimensions and
count are not published. The BOM includes their cost in the hinge price, with approximately 180 screws allowed
across the nine trimmed lengths (both leaves, roughly 50mm spacing plus end fixings and a small allowance).
Do not add separate screw packs to the purchasing total.

The backing and hinge stop 10mm short of both perpendicular edges to clear the remaining bearing lips.
The BOM conservatively allows one 610mm hinge per door before offcut optimisation. Backing stock is
counted from actual moving wood parts. Existing catch hardware remains; obsolete butt-hinge bolts are removed.
