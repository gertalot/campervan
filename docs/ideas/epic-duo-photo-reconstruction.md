# EPIC Duo photo reconstruction

14 October 2026. An approximate top view of EPIC's van, not a modification of the owner's approved layout.

The rear photo shows shallow cabinetry on the left and an L-shaped seating platform on the right, returning across
the front. The side photo supports a toilet under the front return, sliding sideways towards the passenger sliding
door and facing rearwards. Exact rail travel and night configuration remain unverified.

EPIC publishes a rear interior of 145 W × 210 L × 140 H cm, and a bed 210 L × 120/105 W cm.
[EPIC Duo](https://epiccampers.co.nz/the-duo)

Nissan's panel-van brochure gives maximum cargo 150 W × 204 L × 135.8 H cm and rear opening 126.2 W × 122.8 H cm.
These are variant reference dimensions, not measured dimensions of the pictured tailgate van. The difference from
EPIC's rounded listing is unresolved.
[Nissan brochure](https://www.nissan-cdn.net/content/dam/Nissan/gb/brochures/Vehicles/Nissan_NV200_VAN_UK.pdf)
